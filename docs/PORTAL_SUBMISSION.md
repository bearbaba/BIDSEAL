**Title:** BIDSEAL — Consensus-Verified Procurement Bid Gate

**Notes (under 1000 characters):**

BIDSEAL is a reusable GenLayer Intelligent Contract for agent-to-agent and DAO procurement. It binds immutable buyer requirements, wallet-authored vendor bids, and a deterministic maximum price. A GenLayer leader judges natural-language obligations and independent validators rerun the evaluation against the original inputs; they must agree on the consequential ELIGIBLE/INELIGIBLE/UNVERIFIABLE outcome. The contract then enforces a wallet-authorized, one-time award only to an eligible bidder. It rejects self-bidding, duplicate submissions, replay, over-budget bids and ambiguous commitments. The repo includes adversarial unit tests, architecture, limitations and a demonstrable buyer/bidder workflow.
